Spring

When everything began to bloom

Cycled to Regent's Park during lockdown, and stumbled upon the (apparently very famous) cherry blossom lane. The feeling was super magical!

London, UK

Spent our 4th anniversary in London watching Hamilton, but not before playing tourist with Big Ben and a red phone box.

London, UK

Cherry blossom season became our thing. Every year, without fail, we'd go on a sakura hunt together. This is how Yu Zon knows that every year, there's a kind lady who tracks the cherry blossoms in Greenwich Park and shares live updates...

London, UK

Standing under a canopy of pink — our favourite spring tradition.

Summer

Chasing the sun together

We were sitting on the steps of a square in Noto, Sicily with Ling and Heng as a local artist was singing La Donna Cannone and everyone was singing together with him. We can still hear and smell that sweet summer night.

Plitvice Lakes, Croatia

The water really is that blue... blessed to see unreal views together.

Snowdonia, Wales

Hiked up a random mountain in Snowdonia with Nick, Sherman, and RJ. This was our first hike together!

Mt Etna, Sicily

Our first visit to an active volcano. Dark lava fields, dramatic clouds, and wind so strong we could barely stand. Unforgettable.

Autumn

Falling for each other, again and again

We were visiting Hyde Park to catch the last of the autumn leaves in London. GJ had the random idea to go on one of those pedal boats on the Serpentine. Spontaneous activities always turn into core memories for us!

Busan, South Korea

Arms wide open at Gamcheon Culture Village with the Little Prince statue. We felt like kids again.

Lake District, UK

The moment Guo Jie told Yu Zon she was so bad at throwing pebbles she should just leave the lake. Bet you never guessed that backstory ;).

London, UK

Our first visit to Hyde Park in autumn. This is when we fell in love with the season.

Winter

The cold that kept us close

One night it was snowing like mad - we were living with Nick and Krystal in the Olympic Village at the time, and we decided to head down to Victory Park to build a snowman. This is us singing Do You Want to Build a Snowman (which was already kind of YZ's song at this point).

Oslo, Norway

Our first skiing experience thanks to Hedda and Sigurd who guided us newbies. YZ fell approximately 47 times while GJ pretended not to count. Oh, she also crashed into a pizza restaurant at peak lunch hour, but that's a story for another day.

Oslo, Norway

This was when GJ tagged along while YZ was on a work trip in Oslo. We watched one of the most beautiful sunsets ever on the rooftop of the Opera House. YZ felt like she was very creative for thinking of this photo op.

New Year's Day, London

We waited four hours in the cold with friends to watch the NYE fireworks. It was worth every frozen minute — we rang in 2026 together.
