Dress Code: Beach Semi-Formal / Smart Casual

Our wedding will be held outdoors by the beach, so we'd love everyone to dress comfortably while still looking polished.

Ladies: Dresses, skirts, dressy sets, or tailored trousers paired with a nice top — all equally welcome. Think light, breezy, and elegant for a beach setting. Sandals, wedges, or block heels work best.
